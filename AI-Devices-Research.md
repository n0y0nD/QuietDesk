## 🛠️ Open-source / DIY builds (Raspberry Pi)

Closest to what we can build ourselves.

| Project | What it is | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Pocket** | Fully local AI assistant on a Pi 5: Whisper + Piper + camera + touchscreen, 3D-printed body. | [circuitdigest](https://circuitdigest.com/news/pocket-raspberry-pi-based-ai-assistant) |
| **Whisplay AI Chatbot** | Handheld AI chatbot on a Pi Zero 2W, ~$120 in parts. | [yankodesign](https://www.yankodesign.com/2025/08/05/diy-handheld-ai-chatbot-runs-on-a-raspberry-pi-2-zero-and-costs-around-120-to-make/amp/) |
